About Janvi Waterproofing Solutions

Janvi Waterproofing Solutions provides professional waterproofing services for homes, villas, apartments, commercial buildings and other civil structures.

Our approach is simple: identify the source of leakage, select the appropriate waterproofing system, prepare the surface correctly and execute the treatment with proper application methods.
